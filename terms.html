F.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a web server when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your preferences and actions over a period of time, so that you do not have to re-enter information every time you visit. Cookies can be "session cookies" (which expire when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until deleted).

Indo Pakhtoon Tech is committed to proactive compliance with the DPDPA and its implementing rules as they come into force. This section outlines our specific commitments under the DPDPA framework.

N.1 Role as Data Fiduciary

Under the DPDPA, Indo Pakhtoon Tech operates as a Data Fiduciary with respect to the personal data of its users, as it determines the purpose and means of processing such data. As a Data Fiduciary, Indo Pakhtoon Tech acknowledges the following core obligations:

  • Lawful Processing: Personal data will be processed only for lawful purposes with a valid legal basis, including user consent, performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or other grounds recognized under the DPDPA;
  • Purpose Limitation: Data collected for a specific purpose will not be used for any incompatible secondary purpose without fresh consent;
  • Data Minimization: Only data that is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the stated purpose will be collected and processed;
  • Accuracy: Reasonable steps will be taken to ensure that personal data is accurate and up to date, particularly where data is used to make decisions affecting users;
  • Storage Limitation: Personal data will not be retained for longer than is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to any applicable legal retention obligations;
  • Security: Appropriate technical and organizational measures will be implemented to protect personal data against unauthorized processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

N.2 Consent Management

Where consent is the legal basis for processing personal data under the DPDPA, Indo Pakhtoon Tech will ensure that:

  • Consent is obtained through a clear, affirmative action by the user — no pre-ticked boxes or implied consent mechanisms will be used;
  • The consent request is presented in plain, intelligible language, specifying the exact personal data to be processed and the purposes thereof;
  • Consent is not bundled as a condition for accessing the Platform's core utility features (where technically separable);
  • Records of consent are maintained for accountability purposes;
  • Users can withdraw their consent at any time through the mechanisms described in Section E.3 of this document.

N.3 Data Principal Rights Under DPDPA

In addition to the general user rights described in Section E, the DPDPA specifically recognizes the following rights for Data Principals (users), which Indo Pakhtoon Tech is committed to upholding:

  • Right to Information: The right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed and the identities of all Data Processors with whom data has been shared;
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: The right to correct inaccurate or misleading personal data and to erase data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected;
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: The right to have grievances addressed by the Data Fiduciary within a prescribed timeframe, and to escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unsatisfied;
  • Right to Nominate: The right to nominate another individual who may exercise data principal rights on behalf of the user in the event of death or incapacity.

N.4 Cross-Border Data Transfers

Certain third-party services integrated into the Platform (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, and AdMob) may involve the transfer of user data to servers located outside India. Indo Pakhtoon Tech will ensure that any such cross-border data transfers comply with the applicable provisions of the DPDPA and any rules or whitelist notifications issued by the Government of India regarding permissible countries for data transfer. Users will be informed of any significant cross-border data transfer arrangements through the Privacy Policy.
